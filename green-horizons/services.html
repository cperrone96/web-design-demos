What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive lawn care and landscaping solutions for residential and commercial properties throughout Austin and the surrounding Hill Country.

At Green Horizons, every service is backed by 12 years of Austin-area experience, a team of certified horticulturalists, and a genuine commitment to your property's long-term health and beauty. Whether you need a one-time cleanup or a full landscape renovation, we have a solution built for you.

Lawn Maintenance

Our signature lawn care program keeps your turf healthy, dense, and vibrant. We handle everything so you never have to think about it.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly mowing and edging
  • Seasonal fertilization treatments
  • Weed prevention and spot treatment
  • Clippings cleanup and disposal

Landscape Design

From concept sketches to full installation, our designers create cohesive outdoor environments that reflect your lifestyle and complement your home's architecture.

  • Custom design consultation and rendering
  • Native and drought-tolerant plant selection
  • Planting bed layout and installation
  • Seasonal color rotations

Hardscaping

Beautiful, functional outdoor structures that extend your living space — patios, walkways, retaining walls, and fire pit areas built to last.

  • Flagstone and paver patio installation
  • Retaining wall construction
  • Garden path and walkway design
  • Outdoor kitchen and fire pit prep

Irrigation Systems

Smart, efficient irrigation designed for Austin's water restrictions and heat. We install, repair, and optimize systems that protect your investment.

  • Drip and spray system installation
  • Smart controller programming
  • Leak detection and repair
  • Seasonal startup and winterization

Seasonal Cleanup

Start each season fresh. Our thorough cleanups remove debris, prepare beds, and get your landscape ready for peak growing conditions.

  • Leaf and debris removal
  • Planting bed edge cleanup
  • Mulch installation and refreshing
  • Spring and fall pruning and shaping

Tree & Shrub Care

Professional pruning, shaping, and health treatments keep your trees and shrubs looking their best and living longer in Austin's challenging climate.

  • Crown shaping and deadwood removal
  • Deep root fertilization
  • Pest and disease treatment
  • Young tree staking and training

Sod Installation

Instant results with professional sod installation. We prep the soil properly, lay quality sod, and provide aftercare guidance for rapid establishment.

  • Soil testing and preparation
  • Bermuda, St. Augustine and Zoysia varieties
  • Grade leveling and drainage correction
  • 30-day establishment care plan

Lawn Treatments

Targeted lawn health programs including fertilization, weed control, aeration, and overseeding to build a thicker, greener, more resilient lawn.

  • 6-step annual fertilization program
  • Pre- and post-emergent weed control
  • Core aeration and overseeding
  • Soil amendment and pH balancing
