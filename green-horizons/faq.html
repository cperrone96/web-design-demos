Getting Started
Getting a quote is easy and completely free. Simply fill out our online contact form, call us at (512) 555-0142, or send an email to hello@greenhorizons.com. We'll follow up within one business day to gather a few details about your property. For most services, we'll schedule a brief on-site visit so we can provide an accurate, itemized estimate — no guesswork, no hidden fees.
For recurring maintenance plans, we can often start within the same week — sometimes within 1–2 business days depending on route availability in your area. For larger landscape design or hardscaping projects, timelines vary based on project scope, material availability, and current workload. We'll give you a clear start date during the estimate process so there are no surprises.
No long-term contracts are required for our maintenance plans. Our clients stay because they love the results, not because they're locked in. We do ask for a 30-day notice before canceling a recurring plan so we can adjust our schedule accordingly. For larger design and installation projects, we use a standard project agreement that outlines the scope, timeline, and payment schedule.
We serve the greater Austin metro area within approximately 25 miles of downtown Austin. This includes Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Leander, Buda, Kyle, Dripping Springs, Manor, Hutto, Taylor, and all neighborhoods in between. Visit our Service Area page for a full list. If your city isn't listed, reach out — we may be able to accommodate.

Pricing & Billing
Our maintenance plans are priced based on your lawn's square footage, the frequency of service, and which services are included. Our published rates start at $149/month for basic bi-weekly service on properties under 5,000 sq ft, and go up from there based on your property's needs. We provide a clear, written estimate before any work begins so you know exactly what you're paying for.
Recurring maintenance plans are billed on the 1st of each month via credit card, ACH bank transfer, or check. You'll receive a digital invoice 3 days before your billing date. For one-time projects or design work, we typically require a 50% deposit to schedule, with the balance due upon project completion. We accept all major credit cards, Venmo, Zelle, and check.
We offer a few ways to save: new clients receive a complimentary first visit when they sign up for an annual maintenance plan. We also offer a referral credit — when you refer a friend or neighbor who becomes a client, you'll receive a $50 credit on your next invoice. Occasionally we run seasonal promotions; follow us on social media or ask when you call to inquire about current offers.
Our free estimate includes a walk-through of your property with one of our team leads, an assessment of your current lawn or landscape condition, a discussion of your goals and priorities, and a detailed written quote broken down by service. There's no pressure and no obligation. We'll answer all your questions, make our recommendations, and leave the decision entirely in your hands.
