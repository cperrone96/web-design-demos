Service Details

What's Included in Every Visit

Our lawn maintenance service isn't just mowing. Every visit is a complete care routine designed to build a healthier, stronger lawn over time.

  • Mowing at the correct height for your grass type
  • Precise string trimming along edges and borders
  • Sidewalk and driveway edging with a blade edger
  • Weed whacking around fences, beds, and obstacles
  • Blowing off clippings from all hard surfaces
  • Clippings mulched back into the lawn when possible
  • Visual inspection for pest or disease issues
  • Gate and entry latch checks after every visit
  • Post-visit summary texted to you same day
  • Seasonal mowing height adjustments
Our Process

How It Works

Getting started is simple. From your first call to a beautifully maintained lawn — here's exactly what to expect.

1

Assess

We start with a free on-site visit to measure your lawn, assess its current condition, identify any challenges like bare spots or drainage issues, and understand your goals. You'll receive a written estimate within 24 hours.

2

Schedule

Once you approve the quote, we add you to our route — usually starting within the same week. You'll receive a confirmation with your recurring service day and a 30-minute arrival window for each visit.

3

Maintain

Our crew arrives consistently, completes the full service checklist, and texts you a post-visit update. Over time, our care builds a denser, healthier lawn that gets better season after season.
