Pest Control Services

Targeted, effective treatments for every common Austin pest. Transparent pricing — no surprises, no hidden fees.

Our Approach

Treatment That Goes Beyond the Surface

Most over-the-counter sprays kill what you can see. We target what you can't — the nests, colonies, and entry points that sustain an infestation. Every Shield Pest service starts with a thorough inspection and ends with a personalized treatment plan designed for your specific home and pest pressure.

Service 01

General Pest Control

Our most popular service creates a continuous protective barrier around your home, stopping pests before they ever find their way inside. Using an Integrated Pest Management (IPM) approach, we treat the perimeter, entry points, and common harborage areas — then schedule follow-up visits to maintain protection year-round.

Pests Covered

  • Cockroaches
  • Silverfish
  • Earwigs
  • Centipedes
  • Spiders
  • Crickets
  • Stinkbugs
  • Pill bugs

Our Process

1

Full Perimeter Inspection — We identify current pest pressure, entry points, and conditions attracting pests to your property.

2

Targeted Treatment — Exterior barrier spray, crack-and-crevice treatments, and interior spot treatments where needed.

3

Quarterly Maintenance — We return every 90 days to reinforce the barrier, assess new activity, and treat any issues before they escalate.

Service 02

Rodent Control

Rats and mice are more than a nuisance — they chew through wiring, contaminate food, and carry serious diseases. Our rodent elimination program combines strategic trapping, colony reduction, and permanent exclusion work to remove existing rodents and seal every potential entry point against future intrusions.

Rodents Treated

  • Norway Rats
  • Roof Rats
  • House Mice
  • Deer Mice

Our Process

1

Rodent Inspection — We track runways, identify nesting sites, and locate every entry point, including gaps as small as a dime.

2

Trapping & Baiting — Strategically placed tamper-resistant traps and bait stations eliminate the active population quickly.

3

Exclusion & Sealing — We seal all confirmed entry points with durable, gnaw-resistant materials and schedule a follow-up to confirm complete elimination.

Starting at $149

Service 03

Termite Treatment

Subterranean termites are the most destructive pest in Texas, causing an estimated $5 billion in damage annually. By the time you notice the signs, they've often been active for years. Our liquid Termidor barrier treatment and Sentricon bait station system form an unbreakable defense around your home's foundation — eliminating existing colonies and preventing new ones from establishing.

Termites Treated

  • Subterranean Termites
  • Drywood Termites
  • Formosan Termites

Our Process

1

Wood Damage Inspection — Full structural inspection to assess active colonies, damage extent, and moisture conditions contributing to activity.

2

Liquid Barrier Treatment — Termidor SC is injected into the soil around your foundation, creating an invisible transfer-effect zone that eliminates the colony.

3

Annual Warranty & Monitoring — We install bait stations and provide annual inspection and re-treatment warranty for ongoing protection.

Starting at $299

Service 04

Bed Bug Treatment

Bed bugs are among the most psychologically distressing pest problems a homeowner can face — and the hardest to eliminate without professional help. Our treatment combines targeted chemical application with heat-assisted techniques to eliminate bed bugs in every life stage, including eggs, which are immune to most sprays alone.

What We Target

  • Adult bed bugs
  • Nymphs (all 5 stages)
  • Eggs
  • All harborage areas

Our Process

1

Comprehensive Bed Bug Inspection — We inspect all mattresses, box springs, bed frames, baseboards, outlets, and furniture for signs of activity.

2

Multi-Treatment Protocol — Combination of residual chemical spray, contact kill aerosol, and targeted heat application to harborage areas.

3

14-Day Follow-Up — We return to re-treat and confirm elimination. Encasements and monitoring interceptors are included for 90 days.

Starting at $249

Service 05

Mosquito Control

Austin's warm climate means mosquito season runs nearly year-round. Our monthly backyard treatment dramatically reduces adult mosquito populations and eliminates breeding sites so you can actually enjoy your outdoor space. No contracts — cancel anytime.

What We Address

  • Adult mosquitoes
  • Larvae breeding sites
  • Standing water sources
  • Resting vegetation

Our Process

1

Yard Assessment — We identify breeding sites: standing water, dense vegetation, shaded areas where mosquitoes rest and reproduce.

2

Backyard Treatment — We apply a fine mist to all vegetation, structures, and breeding sites using botanical or synthetic pyrethroid products based on your preference.

3

Monthly Maintenance Visits — We return each month during season to re-treat, as new mosquitoes emerge from surrounding areas continuously.

Monthly starting at $79 /mo

Service 06

Ant & Roach Treatment

Ants and cockroaches are the two most common household pests in Central Texas — and two of the hardest to eliminate without knowing the species. Fire ants require different treatment than carpenter ants. German cockroaches behave differently than American roaches. We identify the exact species before we treat, ensuring maximum effectiveness with minimum product use.

Species We Target

  • Fire Ants
  • Carpenter Ants
  • Odorous House Ants
  • German Cockroaches
  • American Cockroaches
  • Smoky Brown Roaches

Our Process

1

Species Identification — Correct identification determines the right bait formulation, treatment zones, and follow-up schedule for complete colony elimination.

2

Colony-Targeted Treatment — We apply gel bait, granular bait, or liquid treatment depending on species, targeting foraging trails back to the colony source.

3

Two-Week Re-Check — We return to confirm colony collapse and address any surviving satellite colonies or new activity before they can re-establish.

Starting at $89
