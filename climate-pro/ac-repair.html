Warning Signs

Is Your AC Telling You It Needs Help?

In Austin's heat, an AC problem can go from minor inconvenience to emergency fast. Don't ignore these signs:

  • Warm or room-temperature air blowing from vents
  • System runs constantly but can't reach set temperature
  • Unusual sounds: banging, rattling, grinding, or squealing
  • Ice forming on the unit or refrigerant lines
  • Water pooling or dripping around the indoor unit
  • Musty or burning odor when the system runs
  • Electric bill spikes with no change in usage habits
  • System short-cycling (turning on and off rapidly)

What We Fix

Common AC Problems We Repair

Capacitor Failure

The most common AC repair. Capacitors start the motors. When they fail, your outdoor unit won't start. Quick replacement, typically $150–$250.

Low Refrigerant

Refrigerant doesn't "run out" — low levels indicate a leak. We find and fix the leak, then recharge to factory spec. Proper weight and type are critical.

Dirty Evaporator or Condenser Coils

Dirty coils reduce heat transfer efficiency by up to 30%, causing the system to work harder and run longer. Regular cleaning prevents this.

Contactor Problems

Contactors control power to the compressor and condenser fan. Pitting or burning causes intermittent or no operation. Fast fix, $150–$250.

Blower Motor Failure

The blower moves air through your ductwork. A failing motor reduces airflow, causing poor cooling and evaporator coil freeze-ups. Cost: $350–$600.

Condensate Drain Clogs

Austin's humidity means heavy condensate. Clogged drains cause water backup, triggering safety shutoffs or overflow damage. Easy preventive fix.
