Warning Signs

Is Your Heater Ready for Winter?

Austin's heating season is short but can hit hard. Don't discover these problems on the first cold night:

  • Furnace runs but produces little or no heat
  • System won't ignite or relights repeatedly
  • Yellow or orange pilot light (should be blue)
  • Burning smell when heat first turns on
  • Unusual noises: banging, rattling, popping
  • Uneven heating — some rooms cold, others warm
  • System short-cycling or running nonstop
  • Sudden spike in gas or electric bills
Smell Gas? Act Immediately.

Leave the house immediately, don't use any switches, and call your gas company. Then call us for emergency heating service once the area is cleared.

What We Service

All Heating Systems, All Brands

Gas Furnaces

The most common heating system in Austin. We repair and install gas furnaces from all major brands. Common issues: igniter failure, heat exchanger cracks, pressure switches, and flame sensors.

Heat Pumps

Heat pumps provide both heating and cooling. Austin's mild winters make them highly efficient here. We service all types including standard split systems, mini-splits, and dual-fuel setups.

Electric Strip Heat

Many Austin homes use electric air handler systems with heating strips as backup. We repair, replace, and upgrade electric heat elements for efficiency and safety.
