Our Story

Austin's HVAC Company Since 2009

We started with one truck and a promise: show up on time, fix it right, charge a fair price. Seventeen years later, that's still the whole business model.

Who We Are

Family-owned. Licensed. Accountable.

Climate Control Pro was founded by Marcus and Diane Reyes after Marcus spent twelve years as a lead HVAC technician for a national franchise. He got tired of upsell quotas and two-week appointment windows. Austin homeowners deserved better.

Today we operate a fleet of 11 fully stocked service vehicles across Central Texas. Every technician on our team is NATE-certified and background-checked. We carry a Texas HVAC contractor license (TACL B123456C) and $2 million in general liability insurance.

We don't subcontract. The tech who answers your service call is a Climate Control Pro employee — trained by us, insured by us, accountable to us.
