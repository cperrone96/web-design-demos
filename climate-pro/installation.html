Is It Time?

Signs Your System Needs Replacing

Repair costs add up fast on aging equipment. Here are the clearest signs that replacement is the smarter financial move:

  • System is 15+ years old — approaching or past expected lifespan
  • Repairs are becoming frequent — spending $500+ per year on fixes
  • Energy bills keep climbing despite no change in usage habits
  • System uses R-22 refrigerant (phased out, increasingly expensive)
  • Inconsistent temperatures — some rooms hot, others cold
  • SEER rating below 13 — modern systems reach 18–26 SEER
  • Facing a major repair (compressor, coil) that costs 50%+ of replacement
Our Process

How Our Installation Process Works

From your first call to the final walkthrough, we make replacing your HVAC system as smooth and stress-free as possible.

1

In-Home Assessment

A comfort specialist visits your home to evaluate your current system, ductwork, insulation, and square footage. We perform a Manual J load calculation to determine the exact system size your home needs — not too big, not too small.

2

System Selection

We present 2–3 system options at different price points with honest pros and cons for each. We explain efficiency ratings, warranty coverage, and estimated energy savings so you can make a confident, informed decision.

3

Scheduling

Most installations are scheduled within 3–5 business days of your approval. We'll confirm a 4-hour arrival window the day before and call when our crew is 30 minutes out. Most installs are completed in a single day.

4

Old System Removal

We safely disconnect, recover refrigerant per EPA regulations, and remove your old equipment. Old systems are disposed of responsibly. We protect floors, walls, and landscaping throughout the process.

5

New System Installation

Our installation crew follows manufacturer specs precisely. All electrical connections, refrigerant lines, drain lines, and ductwork connections are made to code. We pull any required permits — never cut that corner.

6

Testing & Walkthrough

Before we leave, we run a full system test in both heating and cooling modes, verify airflow at every register, and walk you through operating your new thermostat. You'll receive all warranty documentation and a service report.

System Options

Which System Type is Right for Your Home?

Central AC System

The most common choice for Austin homes. A central split system pairs an outdoor condenser with an indoor air handler to cool your entire home through existing ductwork. Cost-effective and highly efficient in SEER ratings up to 26.

Heat Pump

Provides both heating and cooling in one system. Austin's mild winters make heat pumps exceptionally efficient here — they move heat rather than generate it, delivering 2–3x more energy per dollar than a gas furnace in moderate cold.

Mini-Split System

Ideal for home additions, garages, converted spaces, or homes without ductwork. Mini-splits offer zoned temperature control with no ducts required. Highly efficient with SEER ratings up to 30+ on premium models.

Gas Furnace

Delivers fast, powerful heat — important during Austin's occasional severe cold snaps. Modern gas furnaces operate at up to 97% AFUE efficiency, dramatically reducing heating costs compared to older 60–70% AFUE equipment.
