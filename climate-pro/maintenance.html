Every Visit Includes

Our 12-Point Maintenance Checklist

Every maintenance visit follows a structured checklist. Our technicians document every check so you have a complete service record for your system.

  • Inspect and replace air filter (standard 1" filter included)
  • Check refrigerant pressure and look for signs of leaks
  • Clean evaporator and condenser coils
  • Flush and clear condensate drain line
  • Test and inspect all electrical connections
  • Lubricate all moving parts (motors, bearings)
  • Test capacitors and contactors for wear
  • Measure airflow across evaporator coil
  • Inspect ductwork connections at the air handler
  • Verify thermostat operation and calibration
  • Run full heating and cooling cycle test
  • Provide written report with findings and recommendations
