Everything We Do

Complete HVAC Services

From emergency repairs to whole-home system upgrades — we handle it all with NATE-certified technicians and a satisfaction guarantee.

AC Repair

Your air conditioner has one job: keep you cool. When it fails, we respond fast. Our technicians carry parts for the most common AC failures, so most repairs are completed in a single visit. We service all brands including Carrier, Lennox, Trane, Goodman, and Rheem.

AC Installation

Replacing an older system? We'll conduct a full Manual J load calculation to size your new system precisely for your home. Proper sizing means maximum efficiency, lower bills, and a longer equipment life. We carry top brands with up to 10-year parts-and-labor warranties.

View Installation Services →

Heating Repair

Austin winters can surprise you. When your furnace or heat pump stops working, we'll diagnose the problem quickly — whether it's a faulty igniter, a failed capacitor, a refrigerant issue, or a cracked heat exchanger that poses a safety risk.

View Heating Repair Services →

Heating Installation

Upgrading to a new furnace or heat pump is one of the best investments you can make. Modern systems operate at 95%+ efficiency — meaning less energy waste and lower monthly bills. We'll walk you through every option with no pressure and clear, honest pricing.

Maintenance Plans

Prevention beats repair every time. Our maintenance plans include two tune-ups per year (one before cooling season, one before heating season), priority emergency dispatch, exclusive member discounts, and a comprehensive system inspection and cleaning checklist.

Indoor Air Quality

Austin's cedar and oak pollen seasons are brutal. Our air quality solutions — including whole-home air purifiers, UV germicidal lights, media filters, and duct cleaning — dramatically reduce allergens, mold spores, and airborne bacteria inside your home.

Our Process

How a Service Call Works

We believe in transparency at every step. Here's exactly what to expect when you call Climate Control Pro.

1
You Call or Book Online
Reach us by phone or use our online booking form. We'll confirm your appointment within 2 hours.
2
We Dispatch a Tech
A certified technician is dispatched with a fully stocked service vehicle. We'll call when we're 30 minutes out.
3
Diagnose & Estimate
We diagnose the issue, explain our findings in plain language, and provide a written estimate before touching anything.
4
Repair or Install
With your approval, we complete the work. Most repairs take 1–2 hours. Installations are typically completed same-day.
5
Quality Check & Follow-Up
We test everything before leaving. You'll receive a service report and a follow-up call to confirm your satisfaction.
