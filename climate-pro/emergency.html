What Counts as an HVAC Emergency?

Any of these situations qualifies for our 24/7 emergency line. Don't wait until morning — call us now.

  • No AC during Austin summer heat — especially with elderly or young children
  • No heat during a winter cold snap — temperatures below 40°F
  • Smell of gas near your furnace or HVAC equipment
  • Active water leak from your air handler or condensate system
  • Burning smell, sparks, or electrical issues from HVAC equipment
  • Complete system failure — system won't start at all
